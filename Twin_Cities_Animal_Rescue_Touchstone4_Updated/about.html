About Twin Cities Animal Rescue

Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue was built around a simple idea: animals do better when a community works together. The rescue brings volunteers, foster homes, adopters, and local partners together to give pets a safer path from uncertainty to a permanent home.

Our Core Values

Compassion

Treat every animal and person with patience and respect.

Responsible Placement

Focus on matches that work for both the animal and the adopter.

Community

Work with foster families, volunteers, and local organizations instead of working alone.

A Message from the Rescue

Hear directly from the rescue about why community support, fostering, and thoughtful adoption matches matter.

Community Partners

Our work depends on people throughout the community. We appreciate the veterinary clinics, foster families, volunteers, pet supply donors, shelters, and local organizations that help animals receive care and reach new homes.

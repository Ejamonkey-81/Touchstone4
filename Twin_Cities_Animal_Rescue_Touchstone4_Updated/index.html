Twin Cities Animal Rescue

Helping Pets Find Their Next Home

Twin Cities Animal Rescue connects pets in need with safe homes and people who want to make a difference. We work with adopters, foster families, volunteers, and community partners throughout the Twin Cities area.

See How You Can Help

Our Impact

  • 325 animals moved into adoptive homes in 2025
  • 86 foster placements supported
  • 120+ community volunteers involved

Meet a Pet Looking for a Home

Every animal has a different story. Our goal is to learn what each pet needs and make a thoughtful match with an adopter or foster home.

How You Can Help

Adopt

Give a pet a permanent home when the time is right for your family.

Foster

Provide a safe temporary home while an animal waits for adoption.

Volunteer

Help with events, transportation, outreach, and daily rescue needs.
